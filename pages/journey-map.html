10 Rue de Dupleix: The Fortress of the Pen

pin_drop Lat 11.9338° N, Long 79.8297° E • 1908–1918
FACSIMILE MS-PDY-1910 Ink on Rag Paper
Clandestine Axle Distribution System

When the British Governor in Fort St. George issued arrest warrants under the Seditious Meetings Act, Bharathi slipped onto the night train disguised as a merchant. In French Pondicherry, at 10 Rue de Dupleix, he reconstituted the fiery revolutionary journal 'India'—smuggling copies across the frontier hidden within the hollowed wooden axles of bullock carts and fish baskets.

“எப்படியோ தப்பிவிட்டோம்... இனி எழுதுகோலே நமது பீரங்கி!” — Bharati letter to sister, Nov 1908.
