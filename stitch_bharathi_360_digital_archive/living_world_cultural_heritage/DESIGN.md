## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the convergence of early 20th-century anti-colonial revolutionary literature and 2026 spatial museum-grade web technology. Built around the fierce intellectual legacy of Subramania Bharati ("Mahakavi Bharathiyar"), the aesthetic merges tactile archival history—newsprint broadsheets, torn rag-paper manuscripts, handwritten Tamil ink glyphs, and aged gold leaf—with hyper-modern, fluid cinematic interactions.

The target audience encompasses global culture enthusiasts, academic researchers, Tamil diaspora across generations, and contemporary digital museum visitors who demand emotional depth, historical authenticity, and high-fidelity interactive media.

The visual style is **Cinematic Archival Tactile**. It pairs ink-black depths (`#0D0D0F`) with luminous flame saffron (`#D96A27`) and burnished brass accents (`#D4AF37`). Surfaces alternate between deep, filmic atmospheres and tactile aged paper cards (`#EFE7D8`) that feature torn-fiber edges, fine-ruled register lines, and subtle analog film grain. The experience should evoke reverence, intellectual awakening, and cinematic awe.

## Colors

The color palette is rooted in revolutionary ink and archival warmth:

- **Primary (`#D96A27`) — Agni Saffron:** The color of inner fire, dawn, and Bharati's iconic turban wrap. Used for interactive focus states, primary CTAs, active milestone rings, and incandescent atmospheric highlights.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37`) — Burnished Temple Gold:** Evokes antique brass instruments, gold foil print bindings, astronomical coordinates, and illuminated manuscript borders.
- **Tertiary (`#8B2522`) — Colonial Vermilion / Revolutionary Red:** Signifies national struggle, impassioned poetic verses (*Achamillai*), danger, and urgent archival stamps.
- **Neutral Charcoal Base (`#0D0D0F`, `#141316`) — Ink Stone Black:** The infinite digital canvas, evoking deep printing ink and dramatic darkened exhibition halls.
- **Archival Parchment Tones (`#FAF6EE`, `#EFE7D8`, `#D8CAB5`):** Used selectively for rich historical physicalized panels, contrasting directly against the midnight ink backdrop to give digital surfaces the weight of tangible 1910 newsprint.
- **Text & Ink:** Luminous warm ivory (`#F5EFE6`) on dark surfaces, deep carbon ink (`#111113`) on parchment surfaces, and muted warm sand (`#CFC5B4`) for metadata and secondary labels.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial cadence balancing classical gravitas and modern technical clarity:

1. **Headlines (`Noto Serif`):** Chosen for its authoritative stone-carved serif structure and harmonic compatibility with Tamil script aesthetics (`Noto Serif Tamil`). Large poetic proclamations and section titles evoke vintage printing presses, carrying substantial letter weight and rich contrast.
2. **Body Prose (`Newsreader`):** An editorial serif crafted for extended immersive reading. It renders biographical narratives, historical journals, and translated poetry with warmth, humanist cadence, and high legibility.
3. **Metadata & Controls (`Space Grotesk`):** A modern, geometric sans-serif that anchors dates, timeline numbers, coordinate markers, audio player timestamps, and navigation labels. It introduces a calculated 2026 technological counterweight to the historic serifs.

All small labels (`label-sm`, `label-md`) utilize uppercase styling with generous letter-spacing to mirror museum exhibition cataloging practices.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column fluid grid system engineered for cinematic theatricality and artifact presentation.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with 2.5rem (`gutter-lg`) spacing and 4rem (`margin-lg`) canvas inset. Asymmetrical layouts dominate: manuscript sidebars occupy 4 columns, while hero visual narratives command the remaining 8 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with 1.5rem (`gutter`) spacing and 2rem (`margin`) screen padding. Side-by-side modules collapse gracefully into vertical stacked cards.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns with 1rem (`gutter-sm`) spacing and 1rem (`margin-sm`) margins. Dense vertical milestone timelines replace horizontal multi-decade carousels.

Component spacing adheres strictly to an 8px rhythmic foundation. Interactive targets prioritize ample breathing room to preserve the feeling of exploring a curated, spacious private gallery.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tactile physical depth combined with luminous dark-canvas lighting:

1. **Surface Base:** Ground level is the deep carbon abyss (`#0D0D0F`), textured with a continuous 3% opacity analog film grain overlay that prevents digital flatness.
2. **Level 1 (Glass & Ink Containers):** Glassmorphic containers use `rgba(20, 19, 22, 0.7)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and ultra-subtle 1px warm brass borders (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`).
3. **Level 2 (Tactile Parchment & Floating Relics):** Physicalized aged paper panels (`#EFE7D8`) sit prominently on top of the dark backdrop. They use torn-paper SVG boundary masks and soft, long-throw ambient drop shadows (`0 20px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), 0 2px 8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`).
4. **Level 3 (Illumination & Accents):** Active nodes, audio waveforms, and cursor hover states project warm radial saffron blooms (`radial-gradient(circle, rgba(217, 106, 39, 0.25) 0%, transparent 70%)`), imitating candle glow and antique stage footlights.
5. **Hairline Separators:** Dividers use aged brass gradient rules (`linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4), transparent)`) rather than stark CSS borders.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a deliberate **Soft / Architectural (`roundedness: 1`)** aesthetic. Historically inspired cultural interfaces risk losing seriousness when overly rounded or pill-shaped.

- Standard cards, interactive archival containers, and input fields utilize sharp 4px (`rounded-sm` / `0.25rem`) corners reminiscent of cut archival paper stock, glass display mounts, and historic journal folios.
- Larger overlays and parchment modules feature an 8px radius or organic micro-deckle edging (irregular SVG clip paths mimicking hand-torn rag paper).
- Fully circular forms are reserved strictly for circular historical photographic portrait vignettes, map pin nodes, audio play controls, and compass rose emblems.

## Components

### 1. Cinematic & Archival Cards
- **Parchment Manuscript Panel:** Pale sand background (`#EFE7D8`) with dark ink typography (`#111113`), subtle ruled lines, and archival catalog stamps. Ideal for biographical sketches and lyric translations.
- **Glass Exhibition Card:** Dark charcoal background (`rgba(20, 19, 22, 0.65)`), 1px burnished gold border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`), containing circular thumbnail windows with sepia-toned imagery and high-contrast ivory typography.

### 2. Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Hero Button:** High-sheen Agni Saffron container or dark pill with flame saffron outline (`#D96A27`), glowing 40px saffron radial hover effect, containing crisp ivory text and directional hairline arrows (`→`).
- **Archival Secondary Button:** Ghost style with burnished brass border (`#D4AF37`), uppercase Space Grotesk label, and gold hairline hover fill at 10% opacity.
- **Icon Actions (Audio/Language/Search):** Minimal brass circular badges with hairline icon glyphs and golden hover halos.

### 3. Archival Timeline Milestones
- Horizontal and vertical track lines rendered in oxidized brass (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`).
- Milestone nodes feature circular sepia medallions bordered by 2px burnished gold rings with pulse animations.
- Active milestone rings illuminate in Agni Saffron (`#D96A27`) with glowing historical dates rendered in `Space Grotesk`.

### 4. Soundwave Audio Players
- Embedded within dark glass panels (`#141316`).
- Reactive dynamic golden frequency bars (`#D4AF37`) animate smoothly against a muted track bar.
- Features narration voice toggles ("Poet Recitation" vs "AI Historical Reconstruction").

### 5. Constellation Node Connectors & Maps
- Topographic cartography rendered in aged monochrome sepia.
- Geographic nodes (Ettayapuram, Chennai, Pondicherry, Kashi) linked via dotted gold filament pathways (`#D4AF37`) that trace Bharati's historical movement.

### 6. Chips & Filters
- **Category Filter Tabs:** Circular or soft-cornered ghost buttons with glowing gold underline/pip indicators for the active state.
- **Language Switcher (`தமிழ்` / `English`):** Compact pill toggle with charcoal backdrop, gold border, and warm sand text.
