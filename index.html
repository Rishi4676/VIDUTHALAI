1910 Sanctum

Mahakavi's Study in Pondicherry

"Surrounded by police spies and British surveillance just across the border, Bharathi’s writing desk in French Pondicherry was lit by a single kerosene brass lamp."

On this modest wooden table between 1910 and 1918, Bharathiyar translated the Bhagavad Gita into simple Tamil, authored Kuyil Pattu, penned the revolutionary chapters of Panchali Sabatham, and hosted patriot Sri Aurobindo.

Desk Artifacts Brass inkpot, peacock quill, reed pen, handwritten India magazine drafts.
Atmosphere Bay of Bengal coastal breeze, chants of Vedic hymns, freedom pamphlets.
